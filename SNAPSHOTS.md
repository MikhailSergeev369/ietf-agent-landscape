# Snapshot History

This document tracks periodic snapshots of the landscape published to the IETF Datatracker as Internet-Drafts under the Independent Submission Stream. Snapshots exist to provide citation stability; the current source of truth is always `agent-standards-landscape.md` in this repository.

## Snapshot policy

Snapshots are filed approximately every 3-6 months, or ahead of significant community events (IETF plenaries, major BoF discussions). Each snapshot:

- Corresponds to a specific commit of `agent-standards-landscape.md`
- Is filed as `draft-hood-agent-standards-landscape-NN` (increment `NN` per snapshot)
- Notes in its Introduction that the living version lives in this GitHub repository
- Freezes counts, references, and categorization at snapshot time
